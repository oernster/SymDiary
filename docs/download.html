On your phone

SymDiary for Android

Symptoms rarely wait until you are at your desk. The phone app records them where you are, then hands them to SymDiary on your computer, which keeps the whole record and makes the PDF.

1

Open it in Chrome

On your Android phone, open Chrome and go to symdiary.com/app. Type the whole address: if Chrome fills in a different one as you type, remove the extra text before tapping Go.

2

Install it

Tap Install SymDiary at the top of the page, then Install. SymDiary appears on your home screen and opens from there like any other app.

3

Record, with or without a signal

Type the symptom and tap Record now. Until you send them, you can correct or delete events on the phone.

4

Send it to your computer

Tap Send and choose how the file reaches your computer: email it to yourself, save it to a shared folder or whatever you usually use. In SymDiary on your computer, press Import and choose that file.

Use the newest desktop app from this page: older ones cannot read the phone's files. The phone app needs Chrome 157 or later on Android; iPhone is not supported. It keeps your events on the phone, not encrypted, protected by the phone's own lock, until you send them. Nothing leaves the phone any other way: no account, no server and no sync.
